## Español (es-ES)

**Nombre:** Creabel

**Descripción breve**

> El taller de tu novela: capítulos y escenas, personajes, línea temporal, continuidad y análisis
> del texto, todo guardado en tu propio equipo.

**Descripción**

> Creabel es un taller para escribir novelas. Reúne en un solo sitio el manuscrito y todo lo que
> lo sostiene: la estructura, los personajes, los lugares, la cronología y las notas.
>
> Escribe por capítulos y escenas, en un editor tranquilo pensado para sesiones largas. Crea fichas
> de personajes, lugares, objetos y grupos (o las tuyas propias) y enlázalas con las escenas en las
> que aparecen. Dibuja sus relaciones, ordena los acontecimientos en una línea temporal y revisa la
> continuidad para que ningún detalle se contradiga.
>
> El análisis del texto te ayuda a corregir: palabras repetidas, muletillas, adverbios, longitud de
> las frases y equilibrio entre diálogo y narración, con las reglas del idioma de tu novela. Las
> estadísticas siguen tu ritmo: palabras al día, rachas, objetivo y fecha prevista de final. Las
> guías de escritura acompañan cada parte del proceso.
>
> Cuando termines, exporta a Word, EPUB, PDF o texto.
>
> Tus proyectos se guardan en tu equipo, sin cuentas ni anuncios. Si quieres, sincronízalos con tu
> móvil o tableta Android (se emparejan con un código QR) o con tu propio servidor WebDAV (un NAS,
> Nextcloud…), también sin conexión: los cambios se juntan al volver.
>
> En 19 idiomas. Gratuita y de código abierto (GPL v3).

## English (en-US)

**Name:** Creabel

**Short description**

> The workshop for your novel: chapters and scenes, characters, timeline, continuity and text
> analysis, all stored on your own computer.

**Description**

> Creabel is a workshop for writing novels. It brings together the manuscript and everything that
> holds it up: structure, characters, places, chronology and notes.
>
> Write in chapters and scenes, in a calm editor made for long sessions. Create records for
> characters, places, objects and groups (or your own kinds) and link them to the scenes where
> they appear. Map their relationships, order events on a timeline and check continuity so no
> detail contradicts another.
>
> Text analysis helps you revise: repeated words, filler words, adverbs, sentence length and the
> balance between dialogue and narration, following the rules of your novel's language. Statistics
> track your pace: words per day, streaks, goal and expected finishing date. Writing guides support
> every stage of the process.
>
> When you're done, export to Word, EPUB, PDF or plain text.
>
> Your projects are stored on your computer, with no accounts and no ads. If you like, sync them
> with your Android phone or tablet (paired with a QR code) or with your own WebDAV server (a NAS,
> Nextcloud…), even offline: changes are merged when you reconnect.
>
> In 19 languages. Free and open source (GPL v3).

**Product features**

- Chapters and scenes, in a calm editor made for long sessions
- Records for characters, places, objects and groups, plus your own kinds
- Character relationships, as a graph
- Timeline and continuity checks
- Style analysis: repetitions, filler words, adverbs, sentences and dialogue
- Writing statistics: streak, goal and expected finishing date
- Writing guides for every stage of the novel
- Export to Word, EPUB, PDF and plain text
- Everything on your computer, no accounts and no ads
- Sync with Android via QR code or with your WebDAV server, even offline
- Open any page in another window to look things up while you write
- In 19 languages

## Opciones de la entrega

**Restricted capabilities → runFullTrust** (en inglés, para quien revisa):

> Creabel is a desktop application built with Tauri (Win32 + WebView2). runFullTrust is required
> to run as a regular desktop app: it stores the user's projects on disk and, only if the user
> turns it on, runs a small local server so the user's own phone can sync with it over the local
> network.

**Notes for certification:**

> No account is needed. To try it: create a new project, then add a chapter and a scene and write
> in it. Sync is optional and needs another device of the user's own (Creabel for Android) or a
> WebDAV server; it is not required to test the app.
